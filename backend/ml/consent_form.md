# VYRONEX Movement Analysis Study: Participant Consent Form

**Project Title:** Biomechanical Movement Analysis and Real-Time Form Assessment Dataset  
**Principal Investigator / Research Team:** VYRONEX Engineering Team  
**Institution / Department:** Sports Biomechanics & Computer Vision Lab  

---

## 1. Study Purpose
You are invited to participate in a human-subject research study aimed at developing an AI-driven, real-time biomechanical analysis tool for athletic training and injury risk screening. The purpose of this study is to record controlled exercise movements across a diverse group of athletes and gym users to build an accurate, fair, and reliable movement-screening model.

## 2. What Will Be Recorded
- **Movement Tracking:** A camera will record you performing standard bodyweight or light-load exercises (e.g., squats, lunges, push-ups).
- **Landmark Extraction:** The recorded video frames are converted on-device into 33-point anonymized 2D/3D skeleton coordinates (pose landmarks: shoulders, hips, knees, ankles, wrists, elbows).
- **Privacy by Design:** Raw video files are discarded immediately after landmark extraction where possible. No facial biometric recognition, voice identification, or personally identifiable imagery is retained in the training dataset.

## 3. Data Protection and Anonymity
- **De-Identification:** You will be assigned a random alphanumeric Participant ID (e.g., `P001`, `P002`). All dataset files, spreadsheets, and landmark archives will use this ID exclusively.
- **Strict Separation:** Any master key connecting your name to your Participant ID will be encrypted and stored separately on an offline, access-controlled drive and permanently destroyed following data verification.
- **Never Publicly Linked:** Your name, contact details, or identity will never appear in any model training data, publication, or software release.

## 4. Participant Safety & Simulated Form Guidelines
- **Zero / Light Load Only:** All exercises and simulated form variations (e.g., intentional slight knee shift, shallow depth) must be performed using **bodyweight only** or an unweighted PVC pipe.
- **Absolute Safety Rule:** You will **never** be asked to lift heavy weights or perform any movement that causes pain, instability, or discomfort.
- **Immediate Pause:** If you experience any pain, dizziness, or fatigue, stop the movement immediately and notify the researcher.

## 5. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw
- Your participation in this study is entirely voluntary.
- You have the right to refuse to participate, stop a session at any time, or request the deletion of your recorded data without any penalty, loss of benefits, or explanation.
- To request data withdrawal after a session, contact the research team using your Participant ID.

## 6. Institutional Compliance
This protocol adheres to institutional human-subject research ethics and data protection standards.
